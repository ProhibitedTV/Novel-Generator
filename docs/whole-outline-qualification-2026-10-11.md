individual chapter checks. Its saved 24-chapter outline repeated final treaty
ratification, monopoly removal, and first shared planting at chapters 8, 16, and
24. The original draft and database backup were preserved before recovery.

routes. The saved repeated-resolution plan failed, with the required allegations
independently confirmed. A proposal followed by ratification and enactment passed.
Two independent collection pieces about different neighbors resolving fence
disputes also passed. Similar structures in separate stories do not establish
shared-canon repetition.

A new isolated continuation preserved completed chapters 1–3 and rebuilt chapters
4–24 using Gemma 4 12B for planning. One complete spine and six detailed batches
produced the replacement outline. The whole-book review covered exactly chapters
1–24 and returned `progression_coherent: true` with no required defects. Ordinary
pacing validation adjusted only future pacing fields. Preserved prefix plans and
all 7,676 words of their prose remained unchanged.

The replacement assigns treaty ratification to chapter 16, final charm removal
to chapter 17, and first shared planting to chapter 21. The final chapters show
the consequences and difficult shared season rather than ratifying the treaty
and establishing the orchard again. The accepted outline hash is
`6a715bd5d031c79f1c5520cf15e69850940d58282a868d55433f34179ec1ce6f`.
