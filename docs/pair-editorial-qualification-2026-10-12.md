# Local chapter-pair repair qualification

Final validation of *The Orchard of Borrowed Winters* stopped at chapters 16
and 17 because the local reviewer mixed chapter and paragraph numbers in one
integer evidence array. All 24 drafted chapters remained saved.

The repair uses explicit source-reference objects bounded to each chapter's
actual paragraphs. Exact prose is attached by the application. References in
other chapters provide read-only evidence; local edit anchors belong only to the
selected chapter. A real contradiction wholly within one chapter can still be
confirmed during the pair audit.

The accepted chapter progression guides where to repair premature events without
pretending that planned events already happened. Unsupported requests to reorder
chapters are rejected before editing. Stale diagnoses wait for a fresh audit
without spending a repair attempt. Whole-book caches bind to the complete text,
and empty, unchanged, or cycling chapter rewrites receive bounded source-based
retries without accepting or saving rejected proposals.
